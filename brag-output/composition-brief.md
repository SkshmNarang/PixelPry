## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic` / `polished`
- Creative direction: "Cybersecurity forensic thriller trailer"
- Interpretation: Deep dark cyber navy canvas, vibrant electric cyan/green HUD overlays, monospaced forensic data readouts, fast-in snappy cards that hold long enough to read, beat-locked reveals, and zero generic corporate fluff.
- Angle: Innocent pixels hide secret payload files and CTF flags. PixelPry strips away deception down to the individual bit.
- Hook: A pristine image subjected to high-speed laser scanline and LSB bitplane masking.
- Outro / punchline: The recovered flag banner slams onto the screen followed by the GitHub repository badge.

  - Abstract filler shapes
  - Sloppy unreadable text transitions

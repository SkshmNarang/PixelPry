The Wood Wide Web: Mycorrhizal Networks

Beneath the forest floor lies a dense subterranean web of fungal mycelia connecting tree roots across expansive ecosystems. Dubbed the 'wood wide web', this network facilitates mutualistic resource sharing and chemical signaling.

Trees transfer carbon, nitrogen, and defensive alerts to neighboring saplings, demonstrating that forest ecosystems operate not merely on individual competition, but as integrated, cooperative biological superorganisms.
